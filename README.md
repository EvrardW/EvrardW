## Options Pricer

[github.com/EvrardW/Options-Pricer](https://github.com/EvrardW/Options-Pricer)

Six numerical methods for options pricing, each cross-checked against at
least one independent method rather than trusted on its own:

- Black-Scholes, CRR binomial trees, Monte Carlo, Crank-Nicolson PDE
  (hand-written Thomas algorithm), Longstaff-Schwartz
- Barrier options (Reiner-Rubinstein closed form + discrete Monte Carlo),
  Asian options (Kemna-Vorst closed form + control-variate Monte Carlo,
  ~100x variance reduction)
- Avellaneda-Stoikov market-making simulation vs. a naive fixed-spread
  baseline: 2.3x lower inventory risk, 3.6x lower P&L variance
- C++ port with an automated Python/C++ parity check
- 76 tests, CI on every push, two real bugs found and documented in the
  process rather than quietly fixed
